About Us

A neighbourhood favourite since 2010

Our Heritage

Where two cuisines meet

Founded in 2010, The Mule Bar & Grill has been a neighbourhood favourite for over a decade. We blend Italian and Indian culinary traditions into a unique dining experience that you won't find anywhere else in Toronto.

Our chefs are trained in authentic techniques from both traditions, using herbs and spices sourced locally wherever possible. We believe great food is about respect — for ingredients, for tradition, and for the people at the table.

Whether you're a student looking for a warm meal, or a family celebrating a special occasion, our doors are always open. Come as you are.

What We Stand For

Our values

The principles that guide everything from our sourcing to our service

Fresh & Local

We source seasonal ingredients from local Ontario farms, prioritising freshness and sustainability in every dish.

Craft & Care

Every recipe has been developed through years of practice. Our chefs take pride in authentic flavours made from scratch daily.

Community First

We're proud to serve the Progress Campus community and the wider Scarborough neighbourhood. Everyone is welcome here.
