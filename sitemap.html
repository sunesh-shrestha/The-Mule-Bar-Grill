Site Map

Every page on The Mule Bar & Grill website is listed below with its filename, description, and the technologies used.

  • 1
    Home home.html Landing page — hero banner, mission statement, featured dishes, and customer reviews.
    HTML
  • 2
    About about.html Our story, values, and meet-the-team section for The Mule Bar & Grill.
    HTML
  • 3
    Menu — (dropdown navigation group) The "Menu" nav item opens a dropdown with three child pages: Calendar, Gallery, and Menu List.
    DROPDOWN
    • Calendar calendar.html — Monthly events calendar with special dining nights and seasonal menus.
    • Gallery gallery.html — Photo gallery showcasing Italian and Indian dishes, and the restaurant interior.
    • Menu List menulist.html — Full menu listing of all Italian and Indian dishes with descriptions and prices.
  • 4
    Order order.html + order.js Interactive order form — select menu items via checkboxes; JavaScript calculates subtotal, HST (13%), and total in real time.
    HTML + JS
  • 5
    Location location.html Address, opening hours, and embedded Google Map.
    HTML
  • 6
    Contact contact.html Contact form (name, email, message) and restaurant contact details including address, email, and social media links.
    HTML
  • 7
    Sitemap & Credits sitemap.html This page — a complete list of all pages on the website plus acknowledgements for images, references, and tools used.
    HTML
